Advancing In Silico Modeling for Hemodialysis: From Patient-Specific Prediction of Uremic Toxin Kinetics to Hemodialyzer Performance Analysis

Master Thesis — HEAL ITALIA Foundation

Development and optimization of a mechanistic mathematical model to predict protein-bound uremic toxin (PBUT) kinetics during on-line hemodiafiltration, leveraging clinical data to support patient-specific treatment monitoring. To translate these predictive capabilities into an accessible tool, the model was integrated into an intuitive, user-friendly graphical user interface (GUI). Complementing this work, a comparative analysis of two commercial high-flux hemodialyzers was carried out using computational fluid dynamics (CFD), specifically investigating the blood compartment to assess the influence of device design on blood flow dynamics. To ensure a rigorous study, statistical analyses were conducted to process and evaluate the acquired data.

Nota di riservatezza: Dati e report completi sono temporaneamente confidenziali e soggetti a embargo in vista di una prossima pubblicazione scientifica.
